About the Department

Welcome to the Department of Computer Science and Information Technology at the University of Jammu. Established with a vision to create skilled professionals and researchers, our department is a hub of technological innovation and academic rigor.

We offer a dynamic learning environment bridging theoretical foundations with practical applications in areas like Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cyber Security, and Software Engineering. Our commitment is to empower students with the knowledge required to thrive in the evolving global digital landscape.
